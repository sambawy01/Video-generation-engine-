## Voiceover as one continuous read

Tonight, the Moon is ten times closer. And nobody can look away.
The oceans react first. Whole streets vanish under a silver tide.
And humans do what humans always do. They build a rocket.
For children, every crater becomes a story worth staying up for.
Night turns into a second daylight, and neighbours finally meet under it.
One Moon, ten times closer. Would you stay... or run?

Delivery: low, calm, slightly awed, like a nature documentary. Slow down on "ten times closer" in lines 1 and 6, and leave a half-second pause before "or run?".

## Caption style

Bold white sans-serif, one line, centred in the lower third, with a soft shadow. Keep it clear of the bottom 15% of the frame, where the app buttons sit on Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Fade each caption in and out over 0.2 s.
